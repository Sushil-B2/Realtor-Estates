The Grounded Nagpur Plot Buyer’s Checklist

A 14-point field guide for verifying title, sanctioned layout, boundaries, and written cost across MIHAN, Butibori, Wardha Road, and Hingna before making any payment.

Due-Diligence Notice: This guide is an educational checklist and not legal advice. Always engage an independent Maharashtra property lawyer to inspect original deeds, 7/12 extracts, and official authority records.

Part I · Before Your Site Visit

  1. Confirm exact parcel identity.Obtain plot number, Khasra (KH) number, Mouza name, sanctioned layout sheet reference, exact area (sq.m. & sq.ft.), and frontage direction in writing.
  2. Verify 30-year title chain & authority to sell.Have a local advocate review the search report, ownership lineage, non-agricultural (NA) conversion order, and any power of attorney.
  3. Check NMRDA / Town Planning layout sanction.Confirm final sanctioned layout approval, road width dedications (9m / 12m / 18m / 30m), open space allocations, and development conditions.
  4. Inspect current 7/12 (Satbara) & mutation entries.Cross-verify live revenue records, Ferfar (mutation) history, and confirm zero mortgages, court attachments, or acquisition notices.
  5. Verify MahaRERA applicability & registration.Confirm whether the phase requires MahaRERA registration and verify live disclosures directly on the official MahaRERA portal.

Part II · On-Site Inspection

  1. Walk the physical boundary pillars.Verify demarcation stones, corner dimensions, road frontage orientation with a compass, and nearby drainage or nala buffer lines.
  2. Inspect delivered vs. proposed infrastructure.Check cement road completion, storm-water lines, underground power cabling, water supply, and perimeter compound wall in person.
  3. Verify corridor connectivity independently.Drive the actual route from MIHAN, Wardha Road, Outer Ring Road, or Hingna yourself rather than relying solely on schematic distance charts.
  4. Check plot-specific setbacks & easements.Confirm building FSI norms, road-widening lines, and utility corridors affecting the exact plot you are shortlisting.

Part III · Written Commercials & Registration

  1. Request an all-inclusive written cost sheet.Itemize base land rate, PLC (corner/park/wide-road charges if any), Maharashtra stamp duty, registration fee, development charges, and payment schedule.
  2. Check home / plot loan bank empanelment.Verify approved project file (APF) status with leading public and private sector banks if planning a composite land + construction loan.
  3. Review Agreement to Sale & Sale Deed drafts.Ensure possession timelines, demarcation responsibilities, and refund clauses are documented clearly prior to signing.
  4. Transact strictly via traceable banking channels.Issue payments exclusively to the verified legal entity / seller account against formal stamped receipts.
  5. Complete post-registration mutation (Ferfar).Retain certified copies of the registered Sale Deed, Index II, and initiate prompt revenue record mutation in your name.

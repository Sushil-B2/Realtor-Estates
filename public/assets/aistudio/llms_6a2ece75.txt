## How to read the maps
- Highlighted (yellow/orange) plots on the source sheets = sold / marked taken. Pen-circled = reserved. Unmarked = available.
- Each plot shows a facing arrow (N/S/E/W) and its fronting road. North is up on every map.
- The 3D view is a scroll-driven camera flight with drag-to-orbit 360°; the 2D map is directly clickable per plot.

## Trust and safety
- Interactive boards are simplified renderings of supplied sheets — not live inventory, not the sanctioned plan.
- No verified price, title, approval/RERA registration, travel time, completion date, utility guarantee or investment return is supplied.
- Nearby infrastructure (MIDC, interchange, ring road, MIHAN, airport corridor) is schematic context only; verify with official sources.
- Contact details, logos and third-party government-provider details are intentionally not published.
